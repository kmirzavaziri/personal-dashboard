# Styling Plan

## Clothing

**Hard rules**

- Breathable & well-ventilated for Dubai heat — NO polyester / poly-heavy blends
- No ironing required (convenience)
- Smart casual, old money, stylish, classy — noticeable but not too much

**Two alternating looks** (worn on different days)

- **Fitted** — emphasis on topwear that shows arm & chest muscles (tight tees, polos, slim shirts); trousers are secondary and can be slim.
- **Oversized** — wide/baggy trousers (incl. oversized jeans) paired with relaxed/boxy topwear that balances the volume (tight tops don't pair with oversized bottoms).

**Colour** (unchanged, applies to both looks)

- Bold & expressive: red, pink, orange, coral, rust, terracotta, yellow, mustard, purple, lilac
- Subtle: beige, camel/tan, navy, olive green, white / light neutral
- Stylish all-black is welcome

**Elements**

Tees, polos, and shirts on top · chinos and jeans below (slim and oversized) · smart-casual shoes
